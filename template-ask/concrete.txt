Build it as a top-down, single-screen Pac-Man-style arcade collect-and-chase game: a tiny ghost haunting a bakery at night, with one float/move verb and one scare verb. On screen are bakery counters as obstacles, browsing customers on simple patrol paths, a scare radius with cooldown, dropped pastries from frightened customers, a pastry count/score, and a sunrise clock filling toward dawn. Scaring a customer makes them flee and drop their pastry; floating over the pastry steals it into the count. Fill the pastry quota before sunrise to win; if the sunrise clock reaches dawn first, the night is lost. High detail.
