## a platformer where you play as a frog

1. **static pond level** (20 turns): Create index.html, css/game.css, and a 480x270 canvas with image-rendering: pixelated, PixelFont fallback, and the 12-color palette variables. Load Level 1 "Ribbon Reeds" from the Level record and the Level roster: 40x17 tiles, 640x272 px, 3 spiky reeds, 5 flies, and exit. Draw static pond art only: water dark #17465F/light #2D7790, solid lily pads/logs #7A00FF/#A855F7 with #0B1D2A outlines, and the cyan exit ring #00E5FF/#F7F7F7. No player, input, camera, simulation, or animation.
   - done: The page shows a fixed 480x270 pixel-art pond level with lily pads, water, and an exit, but nothing can be controlled.

Implement player.js with Pip's 10x12 hitbox and 16x16 sprite offset x -3, y -4, facing, idle/run animation. Implement horizontal platforming only: ground acceleration 1500 px/s², air acceleration 1000 px/s², ground friction 1800 px/s², air drag 200 px/s², clamp 180 px/s, and X/Y AABB collision against solids so Pip can walk on Level 1.
   - done: Pressing arrows/A/D moves Pip left and right along platforms with idle/run animation.

Update player state to jump/fall/hurt and reset player fields from the Player record.
   - done: Pip can jump, land, short-hop, and fall using the specified air control and jump/fall states.
   - reads: ## 1.1 Units, axes, frames; ## 1.2 Important conventions; ## 4.2 Platforming (Tier 1); ### Player record; ## Player states and animations

   - reads: ## 1.1 Units, axes, frames; ## 1.2 Important conventions; ### Level record; ## 4.7 Camera (Tier 1)

   - done: Touching a spiky reed puts Pip in hurt state and restarts Level 1 after 0.9 seconds.
