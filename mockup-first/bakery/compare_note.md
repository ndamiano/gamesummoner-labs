The first picture is one example moment of what the finished game's main play screen should look like. The pictures after it are the game as it is now: its title screen, then play.

Look at them side by side the way a player would. The example sets the style, how each kind of thing is drawn, and the HUD; it does not set the level's layout, where things stand or how many there are, and it is not what the other screens should contain. So find the differences of these kinds: a thing drawn as a box or a blob where the example shows a character or an object; a HUD element the example does not have, or one it has that is missing; text that overlaps, is cut off or cannot be read; a screen that does not share the example's style; a layout that wastes the screen. Fix them, biggest first, as many as your turns allow.

Keep every rule, every number and every level layout exactly as it is. Add nothing the example does not show.
