Midnight Ghost Bakery is a silly side-on stealth game. You are a tiny ghost stealing pastries from a bakery before sunrise so the bakery’s midnight customers never learn what happened. The player is the Ghost. The placed things are Bakery props, including counters, ovens, sacks, and hiding spots. The moving people are Sleepy customers. The items are Pastries. The objective counter is the Pastry counter. The countdown is the Sunrise timer. The detection meter is the Suspicion meter. The scare action is Fright pulse. The grab action is Grab. The level is the Bakery level. The start is the Start screen and Level-start screen. The end is the Win and loss screens.
