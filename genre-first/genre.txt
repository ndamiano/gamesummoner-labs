Someone asked for this game:

{ask}

Do not design their game. Name the genre it belongs to, then describe the generic game of that
genre: the one every game of the genre is, before any theme is put on it. Write it the way a
person who has played a hundred of them would, as the list of things every one of them has.

Write exactly these headings and nothing else:

# 0. CONCEPT

One paragraph, 150 words at most. The genre by its common name. The view. What the player does,
turn by turn or second by second. What is on the screen. How it is won and how it is lost. Name
the kinds of things in it with the plainest generic words there are (a tower, a walker, a path,
a wave): no theme, no setting, no name, no word from the ask.

# 1. WHAT EVERY ONE HAS

A list, one line each, of the features every game of this genre has and a player expects: the
screens, the economy, the progression, the choices, the feedback, the numbers (how many kinds,
how many levels, how long a session). Each line is a thing a player would miss if it were absent.
Fifteen to twenty-five lines. No feature a game of this genre does not need.

Do not mention the ask's theme anywhere. The theme is put on later.
