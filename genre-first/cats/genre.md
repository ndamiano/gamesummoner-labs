# 0. CONCEPT

It is tower defense. The view is a fixed map seen from above or at an angle, with a visible path, a start point, and an end point. Each wave sends walkers along the path. The player watches, pauses, speeds time, and spends currency to place, upgrade, or remove towers near the path. Each tower has range, damage, attack speed, and a targeting rule. Walkers have health, speed, armor, and resistances. Damage and death are shown by bars, numbers, flashes, and effects. The player earns currency from kills and wave completion. A session is won by surviving all waves on the map. It is lost when too many walkers reach the end, reducing a shared life total to zero.

# 1. WHAT EVERY ONE HAS

- A fixed map with a visible path from spawn to exit.
- A shared life total that falls when walkers reach the exit.
- A wave counter showing current wave, remaining waves, and next wave.
- A spawn point that releases walkers in timed waves.
- A currency earned from kills and wave completion.
- A placement rule that shows legal and illegal tower spots.
- A tower menu with 3 to 8 kinds, each with 3 to 5 levels.
- A range preview when choosing or placing a tower.
- A selection mode for inspecting, upgrading, or selling a tower.
- Attack cooldowns, damage numbers, hit flashes, and death effects.
- Walkers with health bars, speed, and scaling stats.
- Upgrade paths or stat boosts for placed towers.
- Pause, speed, and restart controls.
- Resource, damage, leak, and kill counters during play.
- A map end screen showing win or loss.
- A session of 10 to 30 waves on one map, with a final wave.
