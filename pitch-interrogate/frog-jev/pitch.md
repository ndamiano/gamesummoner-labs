# The pitch

Ask: `a platformer where you play as a frog`

Bullfrog Crossing is a platformer where you play as a small frog stranded in a moonlit greenhouse turned swamp. Your task is platforming across flooded pots, glass shelves, and lily pads, while hazards such as black moss, broken glass, and oil punish any careless line. You are after the pale moths hidden in the dark and the scoring left behind by clean, complete crossings. The game is this: a quiet, rainy crossing game where the pleasure is reading a dangerous room, finding the safe path, and beating what you left behind.
