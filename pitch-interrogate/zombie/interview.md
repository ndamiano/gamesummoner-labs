# The interview

Each line: Claude's question, Claude's guess, Nick's answer in his words.

## Round 1

| Question | Guess | Nick |
|---|---|---|
| Is the zombie slow and the humans fast? | Yes, that is why you need traps. | "I think the zombie is roughly the same speed as humans, but the humans are probably spread out. Traps help to stabilize, meaning there are brains to be nommed at any time." |
| What can kill you besides hunger? | Armed humans who show up later. | Answered in round 2. |
| How do traps catch people? | Bait. | "I think I'd let the build do more than just traps. So it could be like 'build a ramschakle decayed zombie mansion with a pool that's fetid, and a wifi jammer + free wifi sign to bring brains to you.' Think something *kinda* like don't starve." |
| What do you build from? | Junk you pick up. | "yeah 100% junk from around." |
| How does it end? | It doesn't. | "no end is def correct, and you need more and better traps / weapons as time goes on." |

## Round 2

| Question | Guess | Nick |
|---|---|---|
| How do you catch someone as fast as you? | They only run once they see you; come from behind. | "probably dumb AI enables you to catch them, combined with stealth." |
| What does each building do? | Lures, catches or stores. Is the pool only funny? | "agreed, but I like decorating houses, even a fetid zombie one lol." |
| Who are weapons for? | Armed humans who come to clear you out. | "correct on weapons. it starts out the locals just go missing. Then a police person investigates, then more police, then idk, the national guard, etc. escalations happen." |
| Is there day and night? | A day counter at least. | "no day and night. and honestly, not even a day counter is needed. probably you can trigger the 'offensive level' based on how many people have been caught / killed." |
| Ten parts so far. Which three work on day one? | Hunger, junk, one trap with one lure. | "agreed, but we're the designers. A good design doesn't limit itself because the build can't do it. That's a build problem, which we should solve. If our designs aren't being built properly, that's a different problem. I agree on day 1. 'Starve to death, capture people, scavenge junk, build traps'" |

## Round 3

| Question | Guess | Nick |
|---|---|---|
| One meter or two? | One: brains is hunger and health. | "One meter." |
| Do witnesses matter? | Yes, an escaped witness adds more heat than a clean catch. | "Witnesses matter for sure." |
| Where do new humans come from? | Lures bring outsiders, heat brings armed ones. | "I would imagine the map is fairly small, and *all* of the people come from off map. I don't think of it as being a town size (and even if it was, towns can have 10k+ people, so a non issue.)" |
| Two or three more gags? | (asked for his) | "wifi jammer + free wifi, banana stand with a giant stack of money and a pit in front of it (you know, there's always money in the banana stand), Free puppies, but they're actually zombie puppies and drag the people to your fridge, and a brain rot cleaning station that people sit down in and it sucks the brain outta their head" |
