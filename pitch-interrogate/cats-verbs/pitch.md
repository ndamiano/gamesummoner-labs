# The pitch

Ask: `a tower defense game where cats defend a fish market`

You’re the head cat of a wet dockside fish market, keeping it open while gulls, raccoons, and rival cats raid the stalls. You command units, manage economy, upkeep, and multitasking, and you use building and combat to keep the aisles from falling apart as each wave arrives. You decide which stall to save, which cat to trust, and what failure you can afford before the market closes. It is a tower defense game where the thing you defend is a smelly, funny, living place: named cats with habits, fish that spoils, ice that melts, and choices about what kind of ruin you can survive.
