# The pitch

Ask: `a tower defense game where cats defend a fish market`

I’m pitching *The Fish Market Cats*. You’re the forecat of a dawn fish market, using building to arrange cats at stalls, crates, and awnings so combat can turn them into the market’s defense as waves of seagull thieves, rat swarms, and raccoon crate thieves come through the aisles. The catch itself feeds economy: every fish you protect helps the market hold, while every theft darkens a stall and makes the next wave bite harder. When the market is about to break, abilities are the desperate tricks you call for, then scoring records how much of the market you saved. It’s plainly cats defending a fish market, but the market is the target, the supply line, and the stage: the cats aren’t generic towers, they’re the market’s own troublemakers keeping the catch out of thieving claws.
