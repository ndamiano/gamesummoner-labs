# The pitch

Ask: `a platformer where you play as a frog`

You’re a small frog trying to cross a dusk marsh to the old nursery pool on the far side. You hop across lily pads, reed ledges, and half-sunk logs while watching for herons whose shadows mean you need to freeze, duck, or break line of sight before they dive. Fixed hazards like cattail thorns, oil slicks, and snapping turtle jaws punish bad timing, while hidden moonflies reveal secret crossings that can turn a safe route into a riskier shortcut. As night falls, the marsh shifts under you: water rises, pads sink, hidden glows appear, and the path you thought you’d mastered stops being safe.
