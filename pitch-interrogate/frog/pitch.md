# The pitch

Ask: `a platformer where you play as a frog`

You play as Mire, a mud-stained tree frog trying to get home to the hidden marsh after the town drains the ponds. You run, leap, and cling through backyards, rooftops, and flooded gutters, using your tongue to swing from wires, snag keys, and pull loose planks aside. This isn’t a frog that fights or collects shiny things; it’s a small animal moving through a human-sized world, and every crossing is about how to get across without being caught.
