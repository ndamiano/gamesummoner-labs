# The pitch

Ask: `a platformer where you play as a frog`

You play as a small frog trying to get to the moonlit pond on the rooftops, where the other frogs are already calling. You don’t run or fight your way there; you crouch, leap, stick to wet walls, hang from twigs, and swing through rain barrels, gutters, and garden edges with your tongue. Every jump is an arc you have to commit to, and every gap asks you to read the place like a frog: what can I catch, what will break, what is too slick, and what is watching me. This is a platformer about being small, slippery, and alive in a wet world, where the joy is finding the clever, physical route upward when the obvious one would get you swallowed.
