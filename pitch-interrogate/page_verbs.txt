Get your game clear before you pitch it. Write the page for yourself: what the first minutes of play are like, moment by moment; what the player is doing and what is working against them; what happens when it goes wrong; what is still interesting after the tenth minute; the particular things in it; and what this game is not. Write what you see, the way you see it. Nobody else reads this page.

The things the player does in your game are systems from the list below. Name the ones your game is made of by their names in the list, and say nothing about how any of them is controlled or how it works: each is played the way it is played in every game that has it, and the designer writes that. Your game is in its world, the things in it, what happens and how it feels; it is not in a new way of moving or acting.
