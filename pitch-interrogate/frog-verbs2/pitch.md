# The pitch

Ask: `a platformer where you play as a frog`

You play a small tree frog in a rain-soaked rooftop garden, trying to reach the Moon Pool before dawn. You cross gutters, awnings, and wet ledges, waiting in shadow while the heron, the cat, and the garden’s dangers watch; the night changes around you, and hidden rain lily bulbs tempt you away from the safe way. It is not a combat game or a road-crossing game; it is a quiet, watchful crossing game about being small in a place that notices you.
